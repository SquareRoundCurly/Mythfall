The Stonebridge Inn is warmer than the rest of Emberwick. Its beams are dark with age, and its hearth throws long shadows over tables scarred by knives and tankards.

Travelers speak quietly here. Everyone knows something has gone wrong at Crowglass Mine, but nobody agrees on what.
